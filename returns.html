Returns & Refunds

Last updated: 16 September 2026.

For help with a return, cancellation, damaged item or refund, email chrisandrews_1990@hotmail.com.

Change-of-mind cancellations

For most goods bought online, you may tell us that you wish to cancel within 14 days after receiving the goods. After notifying us, you normally have a further 14 days to return the item.

How to request a return

Email us with your name, PayPal/order reference, the item you wish to return and the reason for contacting us. Please do not send an item back before receiving return instructions, because some products are fulfilled from different supplier locations.

Return condition and postage

You may inspect goods as you would in a shop. For a change-of-mind return, you are normally responsible for the cost of returning the goods unless we tell you otherwise. We may make a lawful deduction if the value of an item has been reduced by handling beyond what is reasonably necessary to inspect it.

Refunds

Where the legal cancellation right applies, we will refund the amount due within the applicable legal timeframe after the goods are returned or you provide evidence that they have been sent back. Where required, the refund includes the cost of our standard outbound delivery option.

Faulty, damaged or incorrect goods

If goods arrive faulty, damaged, not as described or incorrect, contact us as soon as possible. Your statutory consumer rights are separate from the change-of-mind cancellation right and are not reduced by this policy.

Exceptions

Some goods can be excluded from the change-of-mind cancellation right where the law allows, for example certain personalised goods, rapidly perishable goods or sealed hygiene goods after the seal has been broken. We will not apply an exception unless it is legally applicable to the product concerned.
