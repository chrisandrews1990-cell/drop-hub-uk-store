Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026.

Who is responsible for your information

DropHub UK is operated by Chris Andrews as a sole trader. For data-protection enquiries, email chrisandrews_1990@hotmail.com or write to 2 Norwich Drive, CH66 2HF.

Information we use

When you place an order or contact us, information may include your name, email address, delivery address, order details, payment status, PayPal transaction reference and customer-support messages. We do not receive or store your full card details; payment information is handled by PayPal.

Why we use it

We use personal information to process and fulfil orders, arrange delivery, handle cancellations and refunds, answer support requests, prevent misuse or fraud, keep appropriate business records and meet legal obligations. Depending on the purpose, our lawful basis may be performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, or our legitimate interests in operating and protecting the business.

Who we share it with

Information is shared only where needed to operate the store and fulfil orders. This may include PayPal for payments, CJdropshipping and its delivery partners for fulfilment and shipping, and Netlify for website hosting and server functions. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy arrangements.

International processing

Some fulfilment or technology providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where personal information is transferred internationally, applicable data-protection requirements and safeguards should be used by the relevant provider.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected, including order support, accounting, tax, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Information that is no longer required should be deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase or restrict the use of your personal information, object to certain uses, and receive certain information in a portable format. You can contact us to exercise a right. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office if you have concerns about how your information is handled.

Website storage and payment services

The shop uses browser local storage to remember the contents of your basket on your device. At checkout, third-party services such as PayPal, Netlify and CJdropshipping may process technical or transaction information needed to provide their services. Their own privacy and cookie information applies to their services.

Marketing

We do not currently use the checkout to sign customers up to marketing emails automatically. If marketing is introduced later, this policy and any consent choices will be updated as required.
