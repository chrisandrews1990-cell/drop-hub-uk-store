Supplier setup

This free starter store keeps supplier connections separate so you can avoid paying for automation until you need it.

Suggested workflow

  1. Choose a supplier and product.
  2. Copy the product title, images, cost, variants and supplier URL.
  3. Add your selling price and delivery estimate.
  4. Edit products.js to add the product to the store.
  5. When an order arrives, place the supplier order using the customer's delivery details.

Automation later

If you later use Shopify or another paid ecommerce platform, supplier apps can automate product imports, stock syncing and fulfilment. This free static version is designed to keep your monthly website cost at £0.
